# antislop audit 002

- Date: 2026-10-20
- Mode: **after** (global preference)
- Scope: casino screens only. `app/games/page.tsx`, `app/games/[game]/page.tsx`, `components/games/{game-view,mines-view,seed-manager,boards}.tsx`, plus the admin casino panel from `021cad1`.
- Direction: `DESIGN.md` exists. Dials ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Note that DESIGN.md still says no light theme ships, but `globals.css` ships one, so both themes are checked.
- Method: source inspection plus computed contrast on the real surface colors. No files changed.

Priority key: **HIGH** = Hard Gate (absolute), **MEDIUM** = Purpose-Gate (technique needs a written reason), **LOW** = Quality Lock.

---

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

**1. R-25, small text below WCAG AA in the dark theme.**
`text-slate-500` computes 3.96:1 on the panel surface (`#0d1117`) and 4.23:1 on `ink-950`, both under 4.5:1. It appears at `app/games/page.tsx:45,80`, `game-view.tsx:235,237,239`, `mines-view.tsx:205,209,216,268`, `seed-manager.tsx:70`, `boards.tsx:117,197`. `text-slate-600` is worse at 2.50:1 (`game-view.tsx:243`, `boards.tsx:69`, `mines-view.tsx:189`). Fix: move small text to `slate-400` (7.38:1 on panel) and reserve `slate-600` for non-text.

**2. R-17 and R-38, two content claims that can be false.**
`app/games/page.tsx:40` says "Ten games" while disabled games are filtered out at line 31, so the count can be wrong. The per-game edge labels (`lib/games/registry.ts`, shown at `app/games/page.tsx:65` and `app/games/[game]/page.tsx:46`) state an exact edge, but the operator edge in `settings.games_edge_boost` can change the real return. Fix: derive the count from the visible list, label the static figure as the game's base edge, and state that site payout settings can adjust returns.

**3. R-27, missing and stuck states.**
If every game is disabled the lobby renders a blank grid with no explanation (`app/games/page.tsx:53`). A zero balance has no deposit path or guidance. The seed panel prints `loading` forever when `/api/games/seed` fails, because the catch is silent and there is no error state (`seed-manager.tsx:68`). Fix: explicit empty state, a zero-balance cue with a link to purchase, and a seed error with retry.

**4. R-03 and R-32, tap targets under 44px.**
The roulette number grid is 9 columns (`boards.tsx:544`) and keno is 8 columns (`boards.tsx:423`), which is about 34 to 38px per cell at 360px wide. The stake quick actions `1/2`, `2x`, `Max` use `px-3 py-1` for roughly 26px height (`game-view.tsx:185-205`). Fix: fewer columns on phones with 44px cells, and 44px quick chips.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

**5. R-14 and R-05, ten identical cards with no hierarchy.**
Every game card has the same size, layout, and badge treatment (`app/games/page.tsx:53-69`). Nothing separates a two-click table game from a multi-step mines round, so the player scans ten equal choices. Purpose for the fix: group by play pace (quick, pick, table, reels) with one focal row for the fastest games, so a player chooses by session length.

**6. R-19 and MOTION 1, decorative stagger.**
`Reveal` with `delay={i * 40}` runs on every lobby card (`app/games/page.tsx:55`), which is motion with no reporting job under a MOTION 1 dial. Fix: drop the per-card stagger. Keep motion only where it reports a value, such as the balance count-up and the win banner.

**7. R-17 and rounding, the win banner shows a raw float.**
`game-view.tsx:106,219` prints `result.multiplier` directly, so a 10 percent edge produces `1.7819999999999998x`, while history rounds to 2 dp (`:242`). Fix: format the multiplier at 2 dp everywhere and include the paid amount.

**8. R-27 and R-32, history has no next action and seeds fail silently.**
The empty history is a bare "No rounds yet." with no cue (`game-view.tsx:239`), and seed fetch failures leave the fairness panel unusable with no retry. Fix: an empty state that names the next step, and a retry control on the fairness panel.

**9. R-05, Mines stacks a redundant Balance panel.**
`mines-view.tsx:199-271` puts stake and round controls in one panel, then repeats the balance in a second panel below. Purpose for the fix: merge the balance into the stake card and give the freed space to round data (mines left, safe tiles, cashout value), which is what the player decides on.

## Follow-up (fixes applied)

All eleven findings were approved and fixed, plus the per-game profit panel.

- **1, contrast.** Small text moved from `slate-500`/`slate-600` to `slate-400` in the lobby, game view, mines view, seed panel and boards. The only `slate-500`/`slate-600` left are icon-only mine tiles with no text.
- **2, false claims.** The lobby count now comes from the filtered list. Every edge label reads as a base figure, and the lobby explains that site payout settings can adjust the final return. The history header now filters to the current game, so it no longer claims to be one game while listing all of them.
- **3, missing states.** Added a no-games open state, zero-balance cues in the lobby, game view and mines view (with a plans link), and a seed load error with a retry control.
- **4, tap targets.** Keno drops to 5 columns on phones, roulette to 6, and every segmented control, target chip and stake chip is now 44px tall.
- **5, hierarchy.** The lobby is grouped by pace (One press, Choose and spin, Round by round) with a full-width Mines card, numbered cards on the gold rule motif, and one focal row.
- **6, motion.** Removed the per-card Reveal stagger. Motion now only reports: balance count-up, spin, landing and the win banner.
- **7, rounding.** Multipliers print at two decimals in the win banner, toast and history. History also shows the net payout or lost stake.
- **8, dead ends.** History has a real empty state. The seed panel has an error and retry.
- **9, Mines.** Balance merged into the control panel, and the freed space shows stake and safe-tile progress. The house-edge note now says base and adjustable.
- **10, micro-labels.** Kept only on changing readouts (balance, current, next tile).
- **11, reasons.** Added a Casino section to `DESIGN.md`.

Requested feature: `supabase/017_game_profit.sql` adds `game_stats_by_game`, `lib/games/stats.ts` exposes `casinoStatsByGame`, the admin GET returns `byGame`, and the panel shows all ten games with rounds, wagers, profit, hold and a 24 hour / 7 day toggle plus an empty state.

Verification: 147 tests pass, `tsc --noEmit` clean, `next lint` clean, `next build` green, and a local production smoke confirmed the grouped lobby, base-edge wording, 44px chips, mobile keno and roulette grids, the admin profit section and a populated `byGame` response.
