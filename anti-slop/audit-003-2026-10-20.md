## HIGH (Hard Gate)

**1. R-27 and C-2, Mines erases the round when it settles.**
`components/games/mines-view.tsx` calls `setActive(null)` on both `busted` and `cashed`. The stage reads `revealed` from `active`, so every safe tile the player opened disappears the moment the round ends, while the mines stay visible. The result panel says "Busted", the board shows a fresh grid. This is the "broken" the owner sees. Fix: keep the final round in state after settle and disable input with the existing `done` flag.

**2. R-17 and R-38, Crash is not a crash game.**
`lib/games/crash.ts` is an instant target bet: one float, one comparison, an immediate payout. There is no live line, no rising multiplier, no cashout decision, and no crash event, yet the lobby and page call it Crash and the controls say "Cash out at". The promise and the product differ. Fix: a real per-player round with a hidden crash point, a live multiplier on a chart, a cashout endpoint, and a loss when the round crashes first.

**3. R-19 and C-2, Plinko's ball does not follow the pegs.**
The ball maps `((x + 0.5) / (rows + 1)) * 100` (`boards.tsx:343`) while the pegs map `((j + 1) / (i + 2)) * 100` (`:371`). The two coordinate systems disagree, so the ball drifts between rows and never lands on a slot. The path also stops at the last peg instead of falling into the slot. Fix: one slot-unit coordinate for pegs, ball, and slots, with per-step easing and a final drop.

**4. R-17 and R-38, the Slots edge claim is false.**
`lib/games/registry.ts:121` says "About 5% base house edge". The reel weights and payouts in `lib/games/slots.ts` return 0.922144 per unit staked: 85.17% from triples plus 7.04% from the two-cherry rule. That is a 7.8% edge, not 5%. Fix: state the real base edge (8%) and keep the site-adjustment note.

**5. R-19 and C-2, Dice and Limbo report motion that is not the result.**
Dice uses `(spin.clock / 14) % 100` (`boards.tsx:89`), which wraps from 99.99 to 0.00 and then jumps to `final + (1 - p) * 40` on landing: two visible discontinuities. Limbo counts from 1.00 for 1.5 seconds (`:149`, `SPIN.limbo` in `game-view.tsx:31`) and only then shows the server number, so a fast result looks laggy. Fix: a continuous dice sweep with one landing, and a Limbo stage that shows the result as soon as it exists.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

**6. R-19, Coin flip, Wheel and Keno move without physical cues.**
The coin rotates in place with no toss arc, height, or shadow (`boards.tsx:228-237`). The wheel is a flat conic gradient with a static triangle, no ball, hub, or labels (`:295-300`). Keno reveals via `floor(p * 10)` with one pop class and no per-ball timing (`:407-440`). Purpose for the fix: each motion should report the object that decides the outcome, so the player reads the result from the object rather than the caption.

**7. R-19 and R-14, Roulette is a disc, not a wheel.**
`RouletteWheel` paints 37 equal sectors in numeric order and drops a static pointer on top (`boards.tsx:474-523`). A real single-zero wheel has a fixed pocket order, a rim, a ball track, and a ball that settles into the winning pocket. The winning pocket gets no visual focus beyond a badge. Fix: an SVG wheel with the European pocket order, numbered pockets, a rim and hub, and a ball that spirals into the result.

**8. R-19 and R-14, Slots is a text strip, not a reel set.**
`SlotsReel` is a 60px window over six words (`boards.tsx:596-619`). All three reels spin at the same speed, stop together, and have no payline or symbol treatment, so the result is read from the caption. Fix: taller reels with drawn symbols, blurred fast motion, staggered stops, a payline, and a win focus.

**9. R-19, the spin timings contradict the games.**
Wheel runs 3.0s, Plinko 2.6s, Roulette 3.2s and Crash 1.65s (`game-view.tsx:29-39`), all on the same linear `clock` for the spin phase. A heavy wheel and a falling ball should not share a rhythm, and a crash round should not pretend to resolve in under two seconds. Fix: per-game spin duration and easing that matches the object's mass and travel.

**10. R-03, fixed-size stages crowd small screens.**
The wheel is `h-56 w-56` plus a pointer above it, the coin is `h-32 w-32`, and the reels are three `w-[72px]` cells inside a `p-4` stage (`boards.tsx:298,228,606`). At 360px these are close to the edge, and the new wheel and reel work will be larger. Fix: responsive SVG and container-based sizing with no horizontal overflow.
