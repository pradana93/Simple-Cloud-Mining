## HIGH (Hard Gate)

**1. R-19 and C-2, the fixed spin minimum delays a result the server already returned.**
`SPIN` sets a 1.3s to 3.2s minimum, and `useSpin` refuses to land until that clock and the server response are both done (`anim.ts:57-61`). Wheel and roulette add another 0.95s to 1.0s of landing (`game-view.tsx:29-39`), so a completed bet can sit for about 4.2s. The motion no longer reports the round at the speed the round happened. Fix: remove the minimum hold, start landing as soon as the response is ready, and keep a short per-object landing of 160ms to 380ms. Purpose: the player reads the result immediately while the object still shows how it got there.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

**2. R-27 and C-2, the result text is gated behind the whole animation.**
The result panel renders only when `result && !spinning` (`game-view.tsx:287`). Even with a short landing, the explicit win or loss message arrives after the board settles, not when the server returns. Fix: show the result panel as soon as the landing starts, keep the board motion as confirmation, and leave the win toast after idle. Purpose: the result is information, not a reveal.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

**3. R-31, the timing reason in `DESIGN.md` will be false after this change.**
The casino section currently says the spin holds until the server result and the landing is short. That explains the old minimum. Fix: record the new rule in one line, no fixed minimum, landing begins on response, and auto-bet uses the same path.
